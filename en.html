Privacy Policy

Jinsei Sugoroku (人生すごろく) / Last updated: October 28, 2026

日本語

Katsumi Sakurai ("we") sets out below how the iOS / Android app "Jinsei Sugoroku" (人生すごろく) and its web version (the "App") handle information about users.

1. Information we handle and why

The App lets people who join with an invitation link, a QR code or a group code play the same board game (sugoroku), each on their own phone. To run the games, our server (Amazon Web Services, Tokyo region) handles the information below. There are no accounts, and we never collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.

InformationPurposeRetention
Player name (a name you type in)Shown to the other players in the same groupExpires 30 days after the group was last used, then deleted automatically (deleted immediately when every member leaves the group)
A random ID created by the AppTo confirm that a request comes from a member of the group (stored on your device; it does not identify you)Same as the group data
Game data (piece positions, money, chosen routes and jobs, event history)To run the game, and to let you continue it over several daysSame as the group data
Notification address (only if you allow notifications on iPhone; a per-device identifier issued by Apple)To notify you through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) when it is your turn and when a game starts or ends. It is never shown to other usersSame as the group data
IP addressTo stop repeated guessing of group codesExpires after about 20 minutes, then deleted automatically

The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Images are never stored or sent.

2. Information other users can see

Your player name is shown to the players in the same group. Inappropriate words are masked automatically, and the host of a group can remove players from it. If you see an inappropriate name, please contact us at the address below and include the group code.

4. Tracking permission (ATT)

On first launch the App may show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you choose "Allow" is the advertising identifier used to show ads tailored to your interests (personalized ads). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", non-personalized ads are shown and every feature of the App remains available. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (consent management)

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) about the use of data for advertising. You can change your choice at any time from "広告のプライバシー設定" (Ad privacy settings) on the App's home screen.

6. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13.
